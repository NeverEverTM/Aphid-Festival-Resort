# Future possible goals
- Golden Settlement as a playable map, a path towards it has been made and it is actually accesible as of now! I'm not sure what would I want to put at the town so this is just an idea floating around.
- More specialized aphid genetics, Chao genetics are very in-depth and I could see that working very well with the traits system we have now
- I do have the idea of implementing more resorts, in fact, most of the logic already supports it. But that's a "very far in the future" kinda thing, my ideas are a Ant Kingdom one, a Termite Capital one, and a Deadlands one.
- Aphid Smoothies! This was thought of just as of early 2026 and basically it would be buffs for you and your aphids. Of course, both are made of different things, aphids don't eat their own dew.
- I'm always open to suggestions for more of this, but in particular, I am very open to contributions, specially related to music as my current skills make it impossible to make a proper OST.
